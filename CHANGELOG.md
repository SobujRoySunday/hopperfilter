## [1.0.0]

### Added

- Right-click an empty hopper while holding an item to fill it with your filter layout, taken from your inventory.
- Sneak + right-click a filled hopper with an empty hand to save its contents as your personal layout.
- Action-bar message listing missing items. Nothing is taken unless the whole layout can be filled.
- Free filling in creative mode (`consumeInCreative`).
- Optional sneak-to-fill mode (`requireSneakToFill`).
- Live-reloading config at `config/hopperfilter.json` with per-player layouts.
- Builds for Minecraft 26.1.x, 26.2 and 26.3.
